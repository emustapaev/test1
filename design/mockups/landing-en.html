How it works

Four steps from a rough idea to an app your customers can use.

  1. 01

    Create

    Tell our AI manager what you need. It writes a clear plan and builds the app.

  2. 02

    Test

    Every feature is checked automatically before you see it, so bugs are caught early.

  3. 03

    Deploy

    The finished app goes live in your own AWS account. You stay the owner.

  4. 04

    Maintain

    We monitor, fix and improve the app after launch. Ask for changes any time.

What working with us looks like

  1. You describe your idea in a chat with the AI manager.
  2. You receive a technical specification and approve it.
  3. The AI team develops and tests the app.
  4. The app is launched in your cloud.
  5. Support and improvements continue after launch.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. You talk to the AI manager in plain language and get answers without jargon. You only decide what you want.

How long does it take?

A simple site can be ready in a few days. Complex products take longer; you get a timeline when you approve the specification.

Who owns the app and the code?

You do. The app runs in your own AWS account, and you hold the access and the data.

How do you make sure it works?

Automated tests run on every change. Nothing is released until the checks pass.

What happens after launch?

We monitor the app, fix problems and add new features on request.

How much does it cost?

The price depends on the scope. Send a request and we will reply with an estimate.
